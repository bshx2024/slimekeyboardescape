⌨️ Keyboard Ghosting, Key Jamming & Hardware Optimization

Because Slime Keyboard Escape is an intense platformer requiring simultaneous directional taps and jumping, hardware limitations can sometimes interfere:

  • What is Keyboard Ghosting? Low-cost membrane office keyboards often cannot register more than three simultaneous key presses (e.g., holding W, A, and Spacebar together). If your jump fails to register, try using the secondary Arrow Key layout.
  • Chromebook Key Repeat Settings: On school Chromebooks, navigating to Settings > Device > Keyboard and shortening the "Delay until repeat" setting significantly improves directional responsiveness.
  • Browser Hardware Acceleration: Ensure hardware acceleration is enabled in Chrome/Edge (under Settings > System) to maintain a silky smooth 60 FPS WebGL frame rate during intense keycap obstacles.
